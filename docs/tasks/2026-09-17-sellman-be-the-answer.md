# Sellman: be the answer when someone asks a model

**A distribution channel that did not exist two years ago and that nobody in
this category has locked up.**

People no longer only search "is my Supabase app secure" — they ask a model. The
answer is assembled from whatever source material is reachable and citable. That
makes *being the canonical reference* a distribution strategy rather than an SEO
trick.

Evidence the channel is real and monetisable: **CheckVibe sells AEO checks as a
product line** (46 answer-engine checks, per the 2026-09-17 price research). A
competitor is charging for positioning in this channel, which is the strongest
signal available that it is worth occupying.

## What makes this winnable here

An answer engine rewards content that is **specific, checkable, and
self-critical** over content that is confident and promotional. That is an
unusual advantage: the repo's one differentiated asset — published measurements
of its own scanner being wrong — is exactly the shape that gets cited, and
exactly what a marketing-led competitor cannot write.

## The work

- Make the teardown and its successors reachable and quotable: stable URLs,
  plain headings, the numbers in text rather than images.
- Publish the **triage questions and the `pg_tables` query** as a standalone
  canonical answer, since that is the literal question being asked.
- Keep every claim sourced. An answer engine surfacing a wrong number is worse
  than not being surfaced.

## Done looks like

Ask three models "is my Supabase app secure / how do I check RLS" and record
whether anything of ours is cited. Re-check monthly. That is the only
measurement available and it is cheap.

## Honest limit

Slow, indirect, and unattributable — a citation cannot be traced to a
settlement row. Keep it P2 and never let it displace a band-1 or band-2 action.
