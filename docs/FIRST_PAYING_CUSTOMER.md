# Taskman First Paying Customer Dossier (#105)

## 1. Customer Type
Boutique digital agency owners and Top-Rated / Fiverr Pro freelance studios handling $3,000 to $25,000/month in gross service revenue across multiple milestone gigs, subcontractors, and withdrawal channels.

## 2. Buyer / Decision-Maker Role
- **Buyer & Decision-Maker**: Studio Founder, Managing Partner, or Sole Proprietor who files business taxes (Schedule C, S-Corp 1120-S) and personally reconciles bank deposits against platform earnings.
- **End-User**: The founder or their freelance bookkeeper/virtual assistant.

## 3. Current Workflow & Workaround Cost
- **Current Workaround**: Monthly manual export of Fiverr CSV statements into Excel/Google Sheets, attempting to reconcile net payouts against Stripe, PayPal, and bank deposit notifications line-by-line.
- **Cost of Workaround**: 4 to 8 hours/month of tedious manual labor (~$200-$400/mo in founder time) plus $300-$800/year in unrecorded, tax-deductible platform fees and currency conversion losses.

## 4. Urgent Buying Trigger
1. **Month-end bank reconciliation mismatch**: Bank balance does not match Fiverr withdrawn balance due to unrecorded currency conversion spread (2-3%) and bank transfer fees.
2. **Quarterly/Annual Tax filing deadline**: Accountant asks for breakdown of gross revenue vs platform fees; founder discovers they cannot easily produce exact fee totals without line-by-line calculation.
3. **Dispute / Chargeback alert**: A chargeback or cancelled milestone occurs, leaving an unexplained withdrawal discrepancy in bank deposits.

## 5. Measurable Value Taskman Creates
- 100% automated matching of earnings, platform deductions, and actual bank deposits.
- One-click export of categorized, tax-deductible expenses (Fiverr 20% commission + withdrawal fees).
- Saves 5+ hours/month and prevents $300-$800/yr in tax overpayment.

## 6. Proof Required Before Purchase
A free instant trial reconciliation on a single 30-day Fiverr statement CSV, highlighting exact detected platform fee deductions and any bank variance down to the penny.

## 7. Pricing Willingness Hypothesis
- **Tier 1**: $19/month unlimited auto-reconciliation and audit exports.
- **Tier 2 (Pay-as-you-go)**: $2.00 per reconciliation batch via Taskman usage metering.
