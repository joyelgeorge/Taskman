# Sellman: the seven findings nobody has been told about

**The cheapest marketing this project owns, and it is already paid for.**

The 2026-09-15 verification pass ended with **seven findings compelling enough to
write to the owner about** — five exposed secrets in public source, two reachable
command injections. Two disclosure drafts are written (`flyrpro`,
`Chalmers007`). The lead queue's own status line says it plainly:

> **Nothing has been sent.**

## Why this is marketing and not just admin

A free, unconditional disclosure is **welcome contact**. It is not a cold email,
it does not need a hook, and it arrives with proof instead of a pitch. Every
other item under the Sellman banner is an attempt to manufacture the attention
this already has: somebody who has just been shown a real hole in their own
product is paying more attention than any reader a blog post will ever reach.

It is also the only free channel where the recipient is **pre-qualified by
construction** — they were selected because they have the problem.

## Cost

Zero. The findings exist, the drafts exist, the counts have been re-derived. The
only input is the operator sending a message.

## Done looks like

One disclosure sent and logged through `scripts/outreach.mjs`. Not seven — one.
The reply, or the silence, is the measurement.

## Rules

- Free and unconditional. The fix is the product; the finding is never held
  hostage (`CLAUDE.md`).
- Re-derive the count from a fresh clone first (`verify-lead-before-contact`).
  The numbers in the queue are two days old and F5 says findings decay.
- At most one follow-up.
