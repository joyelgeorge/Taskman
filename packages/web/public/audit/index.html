Were you actually paid for every order?

Compare what you were owed against what reached your bank. If a payout was earned and never arrived, this names it. Works with any platform's export — and if you run more than one, that is exactly where money goes missing.

Your files never leave this page. The comparison runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and there is no account. You can disconnect from the internet before pasting and it will still work.

1 — What you were owed

Any payout export — Stripe, PayPal, Shopify, Fiverr, Upwork, an invoice list. It needs a date or reference column and an amount. Column names do not matter.

2 — What actually arrived

Your bank statement CSV. Credits only are considered; debits are ignored.

This compares two files and nothing else. A gap is a question worth asking your platform — most often a payout falling outside the dates you exported — not proof that anything was taken.
